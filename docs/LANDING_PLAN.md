# Landing page

The landing page follows the structure of Jira's product page (atlassian.com/software/jira) section by section. Colours, fonts and the retro console theme are AMR Hub's own, and all the words describe AMR Hub. Atlassian's text, logos, photos and customer stories are not copied.

| Jira section | AMR Hub section |
|---|---|
| Header with Features, Solutions, Guides, Templates, Pricing menus, search, Sign in, Get it free | Same menus (Radix dropdowns), search, theme toggle, Sign in, Get it free |
| Announcement bar under the header | Dismissible bar announcing the latest board features |
| Hero: headline left, work email form right with "Or continue with" | Same; the email carries over to sign-up (`/signup?email=`), and the alternative is a passkey sign-in |
| Large animated product demo in a dotted frame with a pause button | Live board: cards move on their own, two named cursors, drag to move, pause button |
| Logo strip | Strip of what's included (we have no customer logos to show) |
| Dark band: typing terminal, "Works with", four steps | Typing terminal, works with (Jira CSV, REST API, webhooks, email, passkeys), Capture, Plan, Track, Ship |
| "Keep your entire team in the know" with button and big screenshot | Same layout with an AMR Hub board and team name tags |
| "More than project management": three tall cards | "More than a board": JQL, automation, dashboards |
| "Trusted by teams": stats panel and testimonial carousel | Stats panel with true facts and a carousel of team use cases (no invented testimonials) |
| "Discover the latest": coloured card carousel | Releases and guides cards |
| Teamwork Collection block | "One workspace for the whole cycle" with a reports screen |
| Dark final call to action | Same |
| Footer | Product, Resources and Account columns |
